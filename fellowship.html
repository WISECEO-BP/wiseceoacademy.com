Three years

The 3-year fellowship arc

A rigorous, progressive journey from foundational digital literacy to public defense and collegiate immersion.

  1. Year 1: Foundation & Digital LiteracyMastering core computing environments, introductory Python, physical computing, and hardware logic using dedicated development kits.
  2. Year 2: Applied Engineering & Milestone CoachingStructured project development, 16-week Fellowship Studio sprints, and personalized academic progress tracking.
  3. Year 3: Capstone, Portfolio & ImmersionAn independent, production-grade web portfolio, a public capstone defense, and regional or campus residencies.

16 weeks

The Fellowship Studio

An intensive 16-week sprint where scholars collaborate, iterate, and move from consumers of technology to creators. Guided by expert milestones, they build, test, and document complex digital projects ready for public review.

Weeks 1–4The Artifacts LabEngineering logs, turning exercises into real problem briefs, clean data collection, and a first mentor review.
Weeks 5–8Portfolio ArchitecturePlanning and publishing a personal portfolio, writing case studies for technical and general readers, and peer code review.
Weeks 9–12Narrative & Personal StatementFinding each scholar’s unique “why,” writing evidence-backed essays, and practicing oral defense and interviews.
Weeks 13–16Applications & Demo DayRecommendation planning, a final application checklist, and a 5-minute public capstone defense for families, educators, and partners.
