The fellowship

How it works

  1. ApplyFill out a short application. A parent or guardian needs to know about it.
  2. OrientationFellows and families meet the team, choose a track, and set goals.
  3. Level 1Short modules and portfolio projects, worked at each fellow’s own pace.
  4. Level 2Labs, simulations, and applied projects that prepare for an exam.
  5. CredentialFellows sit for a recognized badge or certification. We share voucher and aid options.
  6. Career connectionsLive sessions with people in the field, plus help finding internships and apprenticeships.
